Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Iora and Asariko ("we", "our", or "us") handle information when you use the Iora mobile application.

Summary

Iora is designed with a privacy-first approach. Iora does not require an account, and every subscription you track is stored only on your own device — we do not collect or store your personal data on external servers. We do not sell your personal data.

Your use of the app is also governed by our Terms of Use.

Subscription and Purchase Information

Iora is free to use for tracking up to 5 subscriptions. If you purchase premium access to track unlimited subscriptions, subscription and transaction processing may involve third-party platforms and services, including:

  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play
  • RevenueCat or similar subscription infrastructure

We do not receive your full payment card details. Billing and payment processing are handled by the relevant store platform and service providers under their own privacy terms. We may receive limited subscription-related information necessary to determine whether premium access is active, such as entitlement or transaction status.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Iora may schedule local reminders on your device for upcoming renewals or ending free trials, based on the subscription details you enter. These reminders are scheduled and stored entirely on your device — we do not send or route notifications through any server of ours. Notification content may be visible on your device’s lock screen depending on your device settings. You can control notification visibility through your device and app settings.

How We Use Information

We use information only as needed to:

  • store the subscriptions and preferences you enter, locally on your device
  • compute renewal dates and spending totals from that information
  • schedule local renewal reminders where you have enabled notifications
  • determine premium access where applicable

Children’s Privacy

Iora is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not collect personal information from users.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Certain limited information related to purchases or subscriptions may be processed by third-party platforms such as app stores and subscription providers under their own privacy policies.

Examples may include:

  • app store platforms for purchases and subscriptions
  • subscription management providers such as RevenueCat
  • legal or regulatory disclosures when required by law

Data Retention

Since Iora does not store your personal data on our own servers, there is no user data retained by us. The subscriptions you track remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Subscription and transaction-related records may be retained by app stores or third-party billing providers according to their own retention policies.

Your Control Over Data

You can add, edit, or delete any tracked subscription at any time within the app. You can also manage app permissions through your device settings, or uninstall the app at any time, which removes all locally stored data.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to design the app with privacy and data minimization in mind. However, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International Use

If you use Iora from outside the country where our business is based, local laws may differ regarding privacy and data rights.

Future Changes

We currently do not use third-party analytics or data tracking tools. If future features introduce such systems, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will post the updated version and revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Iora after changes become effective means you accept the revised policy.
