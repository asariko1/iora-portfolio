Subscriptions, organized without effort

You're spending more than you think.

Keep track of all your subscriptions in one place. Add them manually, get reminders before renewal, see monthly and yearly spending, and check what's coming up on a simple calendar. Minimal, clear, and calm.

Private by default

Iora works offline. No account, no sync, nothing leaves your phone.

Reminded before it renews

A notification lands before each renewal, with enough runway to cancel or keep it.

Nothing rounded off

Each plan shown in its original currency — no estimated exchange rate to get out of date.

Monthly, yearly or contract

Billing cycles beyond a subscription — a two-year carrier contract tracks the same way as a Netflix plan.

01

Add it in seconds

Pick from recognizable app icons — Netflix, Spotify, your phone carrier — or add anything by name. Set the price, currency and billing cycle once, whether that's monthly, yearly or a fixed-length contract.

02

See it before it renews

A calendar marks every renewal with the app's own icon, on the day it lands. Vodafone, Prime, YouTube and Spotify all show up as themselves — nothing to decode.

03

A warning before it's too late

Anything renewing in two days or less rises to the top as a priority alert, price and countdown included — so the ones worth cancelling don't slip by.
